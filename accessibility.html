Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Our commitment

Miisol Ltd wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We have assessed it ourselves; it has not yet been audited by an independent specialist.

What we have done

  • Each page has a single main heading and headings follow a logical order.
  • Every contact form field has a visible, programmatically linked label and identifies its purpose for autofill.
  • Images have text alternatives.
  • A "Skip to main content" link is available to keyboard users.
  • The rotating cards on the home page can be paused, and they do not rotate if your device is set to reduce motion. Other animations are minimised under that setting too.

Known issues

  • Some decorative animations and interactive cards have not been fully tested with screen readers or keyboard-only navigation.

We plan to fix these issues and will update this statement when we do.
